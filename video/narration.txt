Meet PlainPage, a plain language studio for educators and community organizations. Paste any text and get a readability report computed from real published formulas. Flesch Reading Ease, Flesch Kincaid grade, Gunning Fog, SMOG, and Dale Chall, each score shown with its arithmetic broken open, sentence by sentence, syllable by syllable. The jargon radar flags domain terms from an editable list and offers plain alternatives. The rewrite bench applies deterministic transforms, splitting long sentences, hinting passive voice with a mechanical active version, and swapping complex words from a conservative dictionary, then diffs before against after and re scores everything. The audience dial recomputes every formula against your target grade band with clear pass or needs work verdicts. Everything runs locally in your browser with nothing sent anywhere.
