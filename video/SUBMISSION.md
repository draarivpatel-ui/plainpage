# PlainPage | Code for Humanity

Name: PlainPage, a plain language studio for readable writing

Description:
PlainPage measures how hard a document is to read and helps rewrite it. Paste text and get scores from five published readability formulas, Flesch Reading Ease, Flesch Kincaid, Gunning Fog, SMOG, and Dale Chall, each shown with its arithmetic so you can check the number by hand. The jargon radar flags domain terms from an editable list with plain alternatives. The rewrite bench applies deterministic transforms, splits long sentences, suggests active voice with a mechanical rewrite, and swaps complex words from a conservative dictionary, then shows a word level diff and re scores the result. The audience dial checks every formula against your target grade band with clear verdicts. Two built in samples show the before and after. A bureaucratic notice scoring grade 14.8 and its plain rewrite at grade 2.4. Everything runs locally. Fifty five unit tests verify each formula against published worked examples.
